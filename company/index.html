Company Profile

PT. Matahari Abadi Sejahtera

Sustainable Energy Consulting, registered in Indonesia as PT. Matahari Abadi Sejahtera, is a private Indonesian investment company with over 15 years of experience in the Asian market and a long-term dedication to sustainability. It is superseding Sustainable Energy Solutions, previously CV. Energi Alami.

We look forward to achieving together environmentally responsible energetic performances within your operations, and long term savings on your bills — and for our planet.

— The SEC Team
