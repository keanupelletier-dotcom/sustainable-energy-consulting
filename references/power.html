Power System References

Solar PV, hybrid and UPS installations — from 1.95 kWp to 770 kWp.

770 kWp · Grid-Tie
📍 Ciherang, West Java🗓 2017

Aqua Danone — 770 kWp Grid-Tie Solar PV

Designed for self-consumption via bi-directional meter. Installed on a 24/7 operating factory rooftop of 10,000 m². Expected electricity savings of ~15% year-round.

7.5 kWp · Hybrid PV/Genset
📍 Tanah Lot, Bali🗓 2016

Villa Tantangan — 7.5 kWp Hybrid PV / Genset

7.5 kWp PV modules + 3-phase 9 kW grid-tie inverter + inverter battery charger delivering 22.5 kW to an EverExceed 2,000Ah UPS battery bank, with 40 kVA back-up diesel generator.

1.95 kWp · Hybrid PV/PLN
📍 Nusa Ceningan Island🗓 2015

Villa Aqua Nusa — 1.95 kWp Hybrid PV / PLN

1.95 kWp grid-tie hybrid system with 130 Wp PV modules, single-phase grid-tie inverter and 4.5 kW inverter battery charger supplying a 600 Ah × 12 VDC UPS battery bank.

9.1 kWp · Grid-Tie
📍 Perancak, Bali🗓 2014

Indeso — 9.1 kWp Grid-Tie Solar PV

9.1 kWp monocrystalline 130 Wp PV modules with 3-phase grid-tie Platinum 14 k inverter. Supplied for the Indonesian Ministry of Sea and Fishing.
