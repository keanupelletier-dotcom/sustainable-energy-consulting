Thermal System References

Solar thermal, heat pump and heat recovery installations for Bali's top hotels.

Evacuated Tubes · Closed-Loop
📍 Sumba🗓 2017🛢 1,500 L

Sumba Hospitality Foundation

Centralized closed-loop Solar Hot Water System using evacuated tubes. 6 arrays of 30 tubes connected to a 1,500 L fully isolated storage tank. Resol controller for closed-loop operation. Co-developed with Prosol.

Heat Pump · 130 keys
📍 Benoa, Bali🗓 2010🔑 130 keys

Grand Aston Benoa — Centralized Heat Pump

Supply and installation of energy-efficient central hot water and steam generation system, replacing the existing fuel boiler for 187 bedrooms.

Heat Pump + Heat Recovery
📍 Seminyak, Bali🗓 2009🔑 128 keys

Sofitel (Royal Beach) Seminyak

Centralized heat pump system for hot water and steam with heat recuperation by chiller. Replaced existing fuel boilers for 150 bedrooms. Heat recovery brings system COP to 5+.

Flat Panels · 318 keys
📍 Tuban, Bali🗓 2008🔑 318 keys

Discovery Kartika Plaza Hotel — Flat Panel Solar Hot Water

144 flat thermal collectors (~300 m² total) producing enough hot water during the day to supply 318 bedrooms, kitchens and spas. Closed-loop system with isolated storage.

Hot water savings up to 80% are achievable.
