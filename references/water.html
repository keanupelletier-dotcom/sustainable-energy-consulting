Water Treatment References

Filtration, RO, UV purification and HydroFlow installations across Bali and beyond.

Standard Filtration
📍 Residential & Hotels🛢 Sand · Carbon · Softener

Walter Filtration — Standard Applications

Most common Indonesian filtering units: Sand filter (turbidity), Carbon filter (tastes/odors/chemicals), Ferro-manganese filter (iron/manganese), Softener (hardness / limescale). All fitted with automatic valve control (timer or flow meter) for backwash.

Reverse Osmosis · UV
📍 Residential to Industrial💧 1 m³/h → 18 m³/h

Water Purification — RO & UV

Reverse Osmosis for desalination and drinking water purification (removes salt & effluent) — from compact residential units to large industrial plants. UV lamp sterilization eliminates 99.9% of bacteria and viruses.

HydroFlow · P100
📍 Viceroy Hotel, Ubud💠 Anti-Limescale

Viceroy Hotel Ubud — HydroFlow P100

Anti-limescale HydroFlow P100 installed on the main water distribution pipe, protecting the entire hotel plumbing, boilers and heat exchangers from scale build-up — no chemicals, no maintenance.

HydroFlow · 8 × P60
📍 Waterbom Bali🏊 8 × Aquaklear P60

Waterbom Bali — HydroFlow Aquaklear P60

8 Aquaklear P60 HydroFlow units installed on every attraction closed-loop water system. After 3 months: 100% germ & coliform destruction, 50% less chemicals, no algae, exceptional clarity, zero irritations.

Pool & SPA · K40 / W63
📍 Villas, Hotels & Spas💠 K40 · W63 · J62

HydroFlow HS38 · K40 · W63 — Pool & Domestic

Widely installed across Bali: HS38 anti-limescale on private villas, K40/W63/J62 for swimming pools, pounds and spas. Reduces chemical use by 50%, prevents algae and removes up to 80% of existing scale.
