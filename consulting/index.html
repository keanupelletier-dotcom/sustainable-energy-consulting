Consulting Services

Audit · Engineering · Project Management · Financing — your complete path to sustainability.

Step 1 · Audit

Energy & Water Audit — Four Levels of Analysis

SEC offers a full range of audits for any existing operation and facility: survey, measurements and analysis of all energy and water flows, processes and systems. This is the first step to identify opportunities to reduce energy and water expenses and carbon footprints.

L0

Level 0

Preliminary building energy-use analysis based on historic consumption. Determines whether further analysis is required.

L1

Level 1

Visual inspections, study of installed equipment and operating data. Detailed analysis of recorded energy consumption.

L2

Level 2

Detailed audit with comprehensive breakdown of energy use, first quantitative evaluation of corrections and improvements.

L3

Level 3

Investment-Grade audit based on rigorous engineering study — ready for financing & capex decisions.

Step 2 · Engineering

MEP Design & Systems Engineering

Step 3 · Financing

Attractive Financing for Your Project

We provide attractive financing solutions for large scale and community development projects through our network of international financing entities and partners.

  • Structured financing for utility-scale solar, hotel & resort developments.
  • ESCO / shared-savings models — no upfront capex, pay from savings.
  • Community-scale and regional development programs.
  • Local-currency and cross-border options for qualified borrowers.

Clients are backed by extensive component and service warranty on every installed system.
